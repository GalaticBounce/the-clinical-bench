HealthBench is built from 5,000 multi-turn conversations between a model and a user or healthcare professional.
Its conversation-specific rubrics were created by 262 physicians.
The benchmark contains 48,562 unique rubric criteria spanning health contexts and behavioural dimensions.
HealthBench Consensus covers 34 dimensions of model behaviour validated by physician consensus.
The Medical Board of Australia's recency of practice standard can be met with four weeks full-time equivalent in one registration period, a total of 152 hours.
The same standard can be met with 12 weeks full-time equivalent over three consecutive registration periods, a total of 456 hours.
The Board counts a maximum of 38 hours per week as full-time equivalent.
Mercor states compensation of 50 to 180 US dollars per hour based on expertise and project complexity.
---ARGUMENT---
The work is judgement rather than data entry, which is why the briefs ask for currently practising people.
An Australian clinician reading a platform's advertised rate is reading a number set in another currency and another market.
The thing worth checking before you accept a brief is not the rate but the terms around it.
Confidentiality and the ownership of what you write are settled before work starts or they are settled badly.
