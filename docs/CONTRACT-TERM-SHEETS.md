# Contract term sheets for a lawyer to draft from

Written 11 September 2026 alongside `LIABILITY-REVIEW.md`. Three documents, each as a
list of the terms that protect Clinical Bench, with the reason beside each. Not legal
advice, not for publication. A lawyer should draft the documents; these sheets are so the
brief takes an hour rather than a day.

Throughout, "Clinical Bench" means the legal entity, to be named with its ACN or ABN.

**Which model.** These sheets describe the contracting model. If the introducer model in
`INTRODUCER-MODEL.md` is chosen, section 5 of that note lists how the three documents
change: platform terms for companies, platform terms for clinicians, and a courtesy
engagement template between them. The clauses below on registration, insurance,
endorsement, conflicts, data, non-circumvention and the reliance statement carry over
unchanged; the fee, payment, substitution, direction and liability clauses do not.

## 1. Client terms (master agreement plus statement of work)

| Term | What it says | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Parties and structure | Clinical Bench contracts with the client. The clinician is Clinical Bench's subcontractor. No contract forms between client and clinician | Keeps the model as a business delivering a service, which is the labour hire and SG position we want |
| Statement of work | Every engagement has a written SOW: purpose, deliverable, scope, exclusions, timeline, fixed fee, the discipline and seniority to be supplied, data the client will provide | Fixed scope is the commercial promise and the legal boundary |
| Nature of the service | Professional input for the client's commercial purposes. Not clinical care, not a clinical trial, not clinical evidence for a regulatory submission, not regulatory advice, not a warranty that any product is safe, effective, compliant or fit for purpose | Removes reliance arguments and lines up with the site and every post |
| No endorsement | The client must not use the clinician's name, image, title, registration, the existence of the engagement or any part of the deliverable in advertising, marketing, listings, investor material, submissions or public statements without separate written consent from Clinical Bench and the clinician. Breach is a material breach with an indemnity | The Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code bans practitioner endorsements; the client's breach lands on the clinician and on us |
| Confidentiality, both ways | Client information stays with the clinician and Clinical Bench; the deliverable and the clinician's identity stay with the client | Findings that say a product is unsafe must never travel |
| Data | The client warrants any data it provides is de-identified or lawfully disclosed for the purpose, keeps identifiable data on its own systems, and indemnifies for breach. Clinical Bench and the clinician handle data only as instructed | Patient data in AI evaluation is the largest privacy exposure |
| Intellectual property | Clinical Bench owns the deliverable and licenses it to the client for internal use on payment; assignment available for a stated fee. Clinical Bench retains its methods and templates; the clinician retains their know-how | Clear ownership; a lever to price assignment |
| Fees and payment | Fixed fee per SOW, invoiced on delivery or at milestones, 14 days, interest on late payment, GST | Fixed price is the offer |
| Changes | Scope changes only by written variation with a revised fee | Protects the fixed fee |
| Substitution | Clinical Bench may substitute a clinician of equivalent registration and experience with notice | Keeps the service a Clinical Bench service, not a named-person supply |
| Client obligations | Timely access, accurate brief, a named contact, no direction of the clinician outside the SOW | The "no direction" line matters for the labour hire question |
| Liability | Clinical Bench's liability capped at the fees under the SOW; no liability for consequential loss; for consumer-guarantee claims on business services, limited to re-supply or the cost of re-supply as s64A of the ACL allows | Consumer guarantees cannot be excluded for services under $100,000; they can be limited this way |
| Indemnities | Client indemnifies for endorsement breach, data breach, and use of the deliverable beyond the SOW | Puts the risk where the decision is made |
| Insurance | Clinical Bench holds PI and PL cover; certificates on request | Once the cover exists |
| Transparency reporting | Where the client must report payments to healthcare professionals under an industry code, Clinical Bench will supply payment details with the clinician's consent | Medicines Australia and MTAA members must report |
| Non-solicitation | Client does not engage a clinician introduced by Clinical Bench directly for 12 months, or pays an introduction fee | The commercial protection of an introducer |
| Termination | Either party on notice; the client pays for work done to a milestone | |
| Law and disputes | Queensland law (or the entity's state); negotiation then mediation before proceedings | |

## 2. Clinician subcontractor agreement

| Term | What it says | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Independent contractor to Clinical Bench. Free to accept or decline any engagement, free to work elsewhere, uses own equipment, not an employee, agent or partner | Sham contracting and employment status |
| Contracting entity | The clinician nominates whether they contract personally or through their own company, trust or partnership, with ABN. Payments to a company or trust carry no superannuation; payments to an individual for their labour carry superannuation, which Clinical Bench pays on top of the fee | The SG position, stated openly |
| Registration | Warranty of current registration with Ahpra or the relevant body; duty to notify any condition, undertaking, suspension or lapse within 48 hours; consent to Clinical Bench checking the public register at any time | The site's core promise |
| Insurance | Warranty of professional indemnity insurance that covers the engagement, with certificate on request; duty to notify any change | Ahpra treats advisory work as practice requiring PII |
| Scope of work | Only the SOW. No clinical care, no treatment, no patient relationship, no diagnosis for any individual, no clinical trial activity. If a task would require any of these, stop and tell Clinical Bench | Keeps every engagement outside clinical practice and outside research |
| Standard of work | With due care and skill, personally, in writing where the SOW says so, separating what was observed from what is recommended | The ACL due care guarantee flows down |
| Personal performance | The clinician performs the work personally and does not delegate | Consistent with the registration check; accept that this points toward SG on personal payments |
| Direction | The clinician works to Clinical Bench's brief. The client may provide access and information but does not direct the clinician's hours or method | The labour hire line |
| No endorsement | The clinician does not endorse, review publicly, promote or allow their name to be used for any client product, and tells Clinical Bench of any approach to do so | Advertising Code and National Law |
| Conflicts | Declaration per engagement of any financial interest in the client, a competitor, or an employer relationship; no engagement touching the clinician's own patients or employer without disclosure and approval | Medical Board code of conduct |
| Confidentiality and data | No disclosure, no copying, no retention of client data; identifiable data only inside the client's environment; return or destroy on completion; breach notification | Privacy |
| Intellectual property | Assigns deliverables to Clinical Bench on payment; keeps own know-how and pre-existing material | |
| Fees and payment | Fixed fee per SOW stated before acceptance; invoiced on delivery; paid within 14 days of client payment or 30 days, whichever is earlier; GST if registered | The offer on the site |
| Transparency reporting consent | Consent for Clinical Bench to disclose payments to a client that must report them under an industry code | |
| Non-circumvention | The clinician does not contract directly with a client introduced by Clinical Bench for 12 months | |
| Liability | Clinician liable for own negligence and breach; no liability for the client's use of findings | |
| Termination and removal from the bench | Either party on notice; immediate removal on loss of registration, loss of insurance or breach of the endorsement or data terms | |
| Privacy notice | Reference to the privacy policy for how application and registration data are handled | |

## 3. Website terms of use and disclaimers

Publish at `/terms/` and link it from the footer in place of the dead link.

- The site is general information for companies and clinicians. Nothing on it is legal,
  regulatory, clinical, financial or professional advice, and nothing describes any
  reader's situation.
- Clinical Bench supplies private professional input for commercial work. It is not a
  clinical service, does not provide healthcare, does not run clinical trials and is not a
  contract research organisation. No content on the site is an endorsement of any product.
- Enquiries and applications create no engagement; engagements exist only under a signed
  statement of work.
- Registration checks are made against public registers at the time of matching and are
  not a warranty of a practitioner's future conduct.
- Third-party links are provided for reference; Clinical Bench does not control them.
- Limitation of liability for use of the site to the extent the law allows; the consumer
  guarantees are not excluded.
- Intellectual property in the site's content belongs to Clinical Bench; articles may be
  quoted with attribution and a link.
- Privacy handled per the privacy policy; governing law as for the client terms.

## 4. The reliance statement for every deliverable

Put this on the first page of every report a clinician delivers, unchanged:

> This document is professional input prepared for [client] under a statement of work
> with Clinical Bench. It records the observations and opinions of a registered
> Australian health practitioner using the product or material described, for the
> client's internal commercial purposes. It is not clinical evidence, not a regulatory
> assessment, not a safety certification and not a warranty that the product is safe,
> effective or compliant. It is confidential to the client, must not be published, quoted
> or attributed, and does not constitute an endorsement by the practitioner or by Clinical
> Bench.

## 5. Onboarding fields to add for clinicians

Contracting entity and ABN. Insurance provider, policy type, expiry and whether the
policy covers non-clinical advisory work. Conflict declaration. Consent to transparency
disclosure. Consent to the register check being recorded. Acceptance of the subcontractor
agreement before the first engagement, not at application.
