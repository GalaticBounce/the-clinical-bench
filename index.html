Common questions

What teams ask before they commission.

What is Clinical Bench?

Clinical Bench is an Australian marketplace that gives companies on-demand access to credentialled doctors, nurses and allied health professionals for commercial work: product testing, advisory boards, process and regulatory input, and medical AI evaluation.

Who is on the bench?

Registered doctors, nurses and allied health professionals based in Australia. They are screened for commercial experience, including product, private-sector and regulatory work, not just their clinical specialty.

What can I commission?

Common engagements are product testing and validation, sitting on an advisory board, mapping or explaining a care workflow, reviewing health claims, quality and ergonomics input, medical writing, due diligence, and clinician-in-the-loop evaluation of medical AI models.

Is this clinical research or a CRO?

No. Clinical Bench does not run clinical trials and is not a contract research organisation. We supply healthcare professionals for commercial work that needs their judgement and experience, outside direct patient care.

How quickly can you match someone?

Most engagements are matched within days rather than months. You send a short brief, we come back with who fits and how we would run it.

How much does it cost?

Engagements are quoted at a fixed price once the brief is scoped, so there are no open-ended hourly bills. Send an enquiry with what you need and we will come back with a price.

Do clinicians endorse our product publicly?

No. Clinical Bench supplies private professional input for your team. It is never a public endorsement, testimonial or advertisement for a product.
