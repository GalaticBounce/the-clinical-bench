The work

Paid engagements that use what you already know.

Every engagement is scoped in writing with a fixed fee before you accept. Most of it is remote, and you choose what you take.

Test and validate products

Use a device or app the way you would in practice, then tell the team what works, what is unsafe and what they have missed.

Evaluate medical AI

Review model output, label clinical data and rate answers for safety and accuracy. Ongoing, flexible, done in your own time.

Advise and explain

Sit on an advisory board, walk a product team through a procedure, map a care workflow, review claims, or write clinically accurate copy.

Questions

Before you apply.

Does this affect my registration or insurance?

This is commercial consulting work, not clinical care. You are not treating patients and you are not forming a therapeutic relationship. Even so, check your own indemnity arrangements and any employment or contract terms that limit outside work. We will tell you what an engagement involves before you accept so you can make that call.

Am I endorsing a product?

No. Your input is private professional advice to the company. We do not use your name or image in advertising, and nothing you say is published as a testimonial or endorsement without your separate written consent.

How much does it pay?

Every engagement states a fixed fee before you accept, based on the scope and the seniority required. You always see the number first and can decline.

How much time does it take?

It varies from a two-hour call to ongoing work of a few hours a week. You choose what you accept, and most of it is asynchronous and remote.

Do I need commercial experience?

No. Many engagements simply need someone who does the work every day and can explain it clearly. If you have product, regulatory or teaching experience, we will point you at briefs that use it.

Is my information kept private?

Your details are used to match you to work and are not published. See our privacy policy.
